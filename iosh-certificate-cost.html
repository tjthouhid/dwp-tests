Do this in the admin

Use a named learner. After each step, look at Certificate costs and Activity log.

  1. 1

    Check the three prices

    Open Certificate costs and click Certificate prices.

    E-learning £28.10. Face-to-face £37.20. Virtual classroom says No price yet.

  2. 2

    Order one certificate

    On Order Certificates, tick one learner, then choose Order Certificates. An Excel file downloads and the certificate moves to Reconcile Certificates.

    Certificate costs gains one row. Organisation and course are filled in. An e-learning title, including “eLearning V6”, shows £28.10. Face-to-face shows £37.20. Virtual classroom shows Not set.

  3. 3

    Upload the PDF

    On Reconcile Certificates, or Bulk Upload Certificates, upload the certificate. You can upload it again later. That is allowed.

    Certificate costs stays at one row. Activity log adds Certificate uploaded, with the organisation and course. The amount on that line is blank.

  4. 4

    Order the same certificate again

    If it is still on the cost list, order it a second time.

    Still one cost row. Activity log adds another Ordered line, with your name. That line is a note. It is not a second charge.

  5. 5

    Move it back

    On Reconcile Certificates, tick the certificate, then choose Move to Order Certificates.

    The row leaves Certificate costs. Activity log adds Moved back to order and shows your name.

  6. 6

    Order it again after the move

    Go back to Order Certificates and order that learner again.

    One cost row comes back, at the price used now.

  7. 7

    Try a later price

    On E-learning, click Add a new price. Type a new amount and a start date after today. Read the sentence, then save.

    Rows already on the list stay at £28.10. A certificate ordered on or after that date uses the new amount. Both prices stay on the card, each with its dates.

Export

Certificate costs and Activity log both have a tick box on the left of each row.

Tick the rows, then Export

Activity log needs the tick boxes. Without them, Selected rows has nothing to download.
